AgentRoute

Selective communication for multi-agent LLM reasoning, with matched dense and no-communication controls. Model-level evaluation is available; transport validation remains incomplete. [code & evidence]

Persistent Memory and Residual Robot Safety Risk

Studies whether source revocation restores safe behavior in communicating robots, using simulation and memory-recovery policies. Exploratory foundation and replication evidence is available. [code & evidence]

Preventing Task Interruption Hazards

Action-conditioned supervision and recovery for humanoid tasks. Component validation has been released; later safety and nominal-task evaluations remain ongoing. [code & evidence]

A common measurement harness for comparing safety mechanisms across GPU architectures. [code & evidence]

OptiMoE: Routing-Aware Straggler Prediction for MoE RL Post-Training

Investigates early expert-routing signals for predicting generation stragglers; final scheduling speedup is not established. [code & evidence]
