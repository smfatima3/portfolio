Maryam Fatima

Independent AI Researcher

I study how AI systems can remain reliable, efficient and safe as they scale from individual language models to cooperating agents and autonomous robots.

My research focuses on model monitoring and interpretability, multi-agent communication and coordination, privacy-preserving learning, safe autonomy, and efficient machine-learning infrastructure.

I am open to research collaborations and research scientist opportunities. I received the ICML 2026 Gold Reviewer recognition for peer-review service.

Research

Some questions motivating my current research:

  1. Reliable and interpretable AI. What signals can reveal failures or undesired behavior before they propagate? Related work: R-Metric, recurrent-depth probing.
  2. Multi-agent coordination and privacy. How can agents communicate efficiently while maintaining utility and protecting information? Related work: AgentRoute, PrivacyMAS.
  3. Safe autonomous systems. How can robots detect and recover from unsafe decisions, stale information and interruptions? Related work: robot memory safety, humanoid task interruption.
  4. Efficient AI infrastructure. How can safety mechanisms and model execution be made practical at scale? Related work: operational safety measurement, OptiMoE.

Ongoing Research

Public research artifacts and manuscripts in development. These entries are not publication or acceptance claims.

AgentRoute

Selective communication for multi-agent LLM reasoning, with matched dense and no-communication controls. Model-level evaluation is available; transport validation remains incomplete. [code & evidence]

Persistent Memory and Residual Robot Safety Risk

Studies whether source revocation restores safe behavior in communicating robots, using simulation and memory-recovery policies. Exploratory foundation and replication evidence is available. [code & evidence]

Preventing Task Interruption Hazards

Action-conditioned supervision and recovery for humanoid tasks. Component validation has been released; later safety and nominal-task evaluations remain ongoing. [code & evidence]

On the Same Ruler: Latency, Throughput, Energy, and Unit-Economic Cost of LLM Safety Mechanisms at Operational Scale

A common measurement harness for comparing safety mechanisms across GPU architectures. [code & evidence]

OptiMoE: Routing-Aware Straggler Prediction for MoE RL Post-Training

Investigates early expert-routing signals for predicting generation stragglers; final scheduling speedup is not established. [code & evidence]
